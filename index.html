Privacy Policy

Effective September 23, 2026 · applies to Dinner Ideas (formerly CookCart and Weekbite) for iPhone, version 1.0 and later

Dinner Ideas does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

What stays on your iPhone

Your meal plan, preferences (number of days, servings, time limit, diet, excluded ingredients and allergens), pantry quantities, favorites and checked shopping items are saved in a file inside the app on your device. They never leave it unless you choose to.

When data leaves your device — only because you asked

  • Share / Export: when you share your plan or shopping list, or export a backup, iOS sends the content to the app or place you pick. What happens next is governed by that app.
  • iCloud / device backups: like other app data, the file may be included in your iPhone's backups under your Apple account settings.
  • Recipe source links: tapping "Adapted from…" opens the original website in Safari; that site's own privacy policy applies.

Network

The app makes no network requests of its own. The recipe catalog is built into the app.

Children

Dinner Ideas is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Health information

Diet and allergen filters are a convenience based on ingredient labels in our catalog. They are not medical advice; always check product packaging for allergens and cross-contamination.

Deleting your data

Data stays on your iPhone until you replace it or delete the app. Settings › Reset clears the active data (plan, preferences, favorites, pantry and shopping checks) but keeps a local recovery copy on the device; delete the app (rather than offloading it) to remove everything. Exported backup files are yours to delete separately, and they are not encrypted by Dinner Ideas. Because we hold nothing on any server, there is nothing to request from us.

"Not enough compatible recipes" — why?
Your filters (time limit, diet, excluded ingredients or allergens) leave fewer recipes than meals in your week. Dinner Ideas never loosens your filters on its own: raise the time limit, choose 5 days instead of 7, or remove an exclusion. Fully vegan weeks work best with a 60-minute limit.
How do I keep a dinner and reshuffle the rest?
Tap Lock on the meals you want to keep, then Generate my week. Use Replace to swap a single dinner.
Why does the shopping list say 1 avocado when the recipe uses half?
The list rounds whole items up so you buy what you need; recipe pages show exact amounts for your servings.
Can I use metric units?
Switch the app language to French in Settings to see metric units. US units are shown in English.
Where do the recipes come from?
Each recipe is adapted from a popular US cooking site and credits its source; steps are rewritten for Dinner Ideas. Cooking times are estimates.
How do I move my data to a new iPhone?
Settings › Export creates a backup file; open it with Settings › Import on the new device.
